Kidney Donation Information

Understanding the donation process is an important step before considering living kidney donation.

Living Kidney Donation

Living kidney donation involves a medically evaluated person voluntarily donating one kidney to an eligible recipient. Donor safety and informed consent are essential throughout the process.

Medical Evaluation

Potential donors and recipients require comprehensive medical assessments. Healthcare professionals determine whether donation or transplantation is medically appropriate.

Legal & Ethical Considerations

Organ donation and transplantation are subject to medical, ethical and legal requirements. Any donation should be voluntary, informed and handled through properly authorized healthcare and transplant systems such as Malik Kidney Specialist Hospital.
